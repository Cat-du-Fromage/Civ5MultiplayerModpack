v2 (changes from JFD's release)
--------------------------------------------------------------
UA: Hohenfriedberg March
- Renamed from Konigratzer March (both are famous Prussian military marches, but Hohenfriedberg is more directly attributed to Frederick)
- Golden Age points from combat replaced with Golden Age when a Great General is born (does not increase cost of next Golden Age, half the length of a regular Golden Age).
- Additional XP increased from 50% to 100%. (Prussian units should now always be 1 to 2 levels higher than normal when built)
- Additional XP now applies to Policies as well (Autocracy's Elite Forces).
- Added +50% Production for Military Training Buildings. (Prussia's bonuses scale well but is weak early, this is primarily to help the AI's early game)

UU: Death's Head Hussar (only gifted by city state)
- Added purchase cooldown.
- Now always deals 2HP of damage when moving (previously was a 50% chance to deal 5HP damage).
- If Enlightenment Era for VP is installed, is available earlier at Fortification.
- If 4UC mod for VP is installed, replaces Cuirassier.

UU: Landwehr (replaces Fusilier, or Line Infantry if Enlightenment Era)
- Added purchase cooldown.
- Free Stalwart promotion.
- 4 more combat strength than its replacement.

UB: Kriegsschule (replaces Military Academy)
- 1 Scientist slots.
- Gun and Melee units trained in the city gain the Stechschritt (piercing step in English, referring to the goose step) promotion (+10% combat strength per adjacent gun or melee unit, +1 Movement, Golden Age Points from kill).
- Costs less production, 1000 instead of 1250.
- Available earlier, at Metallurgy (or Fortification if Enlightenment Era for VP is installed) instead of Military Science.
- Yields 5 Science and 4 Culture, instead of 1 Science of the Military Academy.

Events
- Now uses CPDLL's database tables rather than lua to fire (ignore this if you have no idea what this means).
- Potato War choice for brokering peace replaced with Gold and Food cost, scaling with era.

Decisions
- Empower the Junker Nobility bonus to Landwehr production on citizen birth replaced with +15% supply from population.
- Number of units from Award the Pour le Mérite now based on the number of Great Generals, as well as Citadels in your territory. (more AI friendly, as AI likes making Citadels)
- Award the Pour le Mérite now requires at least one Great General or Citadel.
- Award the Pour le Mérite now spawns only your best Gunpowder or Melee unit which are spawned at the capital (receiving experience and promotions from buildings, and Stalwart), and Great Generals are no longer expended.

Misc
- If the Community Events mod is activated, Frederick will use Germany's Unique Events.
- If More Unique Components for VP is installed, gets Hanse as the 4th UC.
